=====================================================================
  FILM TAGGER - INSTALLATION GUIDE (Windows)
=====================================================================

Film Tagger is a free program for keeping a catalogue of the films
on your computer and adding your own tags to them. It runs entirely
on your computer and nothing is uploaded.
Setup takes about 5 minutes. Just follow the steps in order.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 - Download the installer
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Download   FilmTaggerSetup.exe   from the Film Tagger releases page.
You do NOT need to install Python or anything else first. Film
Tagger brings its own private copy.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 - Double-click FilmTaggerSetup.exe
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Double-click the downloaded FilmTaggerSetup.exe.
     - If a blue box says "Windows protected your PC", click
       "More info" and then "Run anyway". This appears for programs
       that are new and not yet widely downloaded.
     - You do NOT need an administrator password.
  2. Click Next, read the licence (MIT, free software), click
     "I Agree".
  3. Choose the options you want:
        [x] Desktop shortcut
        [ ] Start Film Tagger when I sign in to Windows
     (Both are optional. You can always start it from the Start menu.)
  4. Keep the suggested folder and click Install. It takes a few
     seconds and needs no internet.
  5. Leave "Launch Film Tagger" ticked and click Finish.

Film Tagger is installed in your own user folder
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\Film Tagger). There is a "Film Tagger" icon in the
Start menu, and on the Desktop if you chose it.
To update later, just run a newer FilmTaggerSetup.exe. Your library is
kept.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 - Every day: open Film Tagger
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Double-click the "Film Tagger" icon on your Desktop.
It opens in its own window. If the window comes up blank or says it
can't connect, wait 5 seconds and press F5.

Film Tagger keeps running quietly in the background when you close
the window, so it opens instantly next time. To stop it completely,
go to Library & Settings and click "Quit Film Tagger" at the bottom.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 - Add your films (first scan)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Click "Library & Settings" (top right).
  2. Under "Library folders", click "Browse..." and choose the folder
     (or drive) where your films are, e.g.  E:\Movies
     You can add several folders, one per line.
  3. Click "Save & scan". A progress bar appears at the top.

Film Tagger only READS your folders. It never moves, renames or
deletes your video files.
After you add new films, just click "Save & scan" again.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL - Posters and film details (free TMDB key)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
TMDB (The Movie Database) is a free website that Film Tagger can use
to download posters, cast, plot and so on.

  1. Go to https://www.themoviedb.org/signup and create a free
     account. Confirm the e-mail they send you.
  2. Log in, click your profile picture (top right) > Settings > API
     (or go to https://www.themoviedb.org/settings/api ).
  3. Click "Create" / "Request an API Key" and choose "Developer".
  4. Accept the terms and fill in the form:
        Type of use:           Personal
        Application name:      Film Tagger
        Application URL:       http://localhost:5050
        Application summary:   Personal app to catalogue my own film
                               collection.
     (Your name, address etc. as asked.)
  5. After you submit, the API page shows two codes:
        "API Key"                  <- a SHORT code (32 letters/numbers)
        "API Read Access Token"    <- a very LONG code
     Copy the SHORT "API Key".
  6. In Film Tagger: Library & Settings > TMDB > paste it > "Save key".
  7. Click "Fetch all posters". Films it isn't sure about are listed
     under "Show only: Needs TMDB match" (left panel). Open those and
     click "Fetch from TMDB" to pick the right one yourself.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
OPTIONAL - Smart tagging with AI
---------------------------------------------------------------------
AI can suggest tags (genre, mood, themes, setting...) for your films.
Nothing is added until you approve it.

First, choose films: right-click a film (or select several with
Ctrl+click) and pick "Smart tagging (AI)". This puts them in the
queue.

Then EITHER:

  A) Free, using any AI chatbot (no key needed)
     1. Click "Queue" (top bar), then "Copy prompt for a chatbot".
     2. Open ChatGPT, Grok, Claude, Gemini or similar, paste, and send.
     3. Copy the AI's whole reply (the part starting with [ ).
     4. Back in Film Tagger's Queue window, paste it in the box and
        click "Import as pending".

  B) Automatic, with your own AI key
     1. Library & Settings > "Smart tagging with your own AI".
     2. Fill in the Endpoint URL, Model name and API key from your AI
        provider (examples are shown on screen), then "Save AI settings"
        and "Test connection".
     3. Click "Process smart-tag queue", or right-click any film and
        choose "Tag now with my AI".
     Note: AI providers may charge for use. Check their prices.

Finally, click "AI review" (top bar) and approve or reject the
suggested tags.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------------------------------------------------------------
The window says "localhost refused to connect" / "can't reach this page"
   - Wait 5 seconds and press F5. The first start can be slow.
   - Still not working? Press Windows+R, paste this and press Enter:
         cmd /k cd /d "%LOCALAPPDATA%\Film Tagger" && set "FILMTAGGER_DB=data\films.db" && python\python.exe app.py
     Any error message appears in that window.
   - Another program already uses port 5050? Put a different number
     (e.g. 5051) in the file  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Film Tagger\data\port.txt
     and start Film Tagger again.

A Windows Firewall box appears
   Film Tagger only talks to your own computer. Clicking "Cancel" or
   "Allow" both work.

Some films have the wrong title
   Click the film and correct it, or use "Fetch from TMDB" to choose
   the right match.

The drive with my films is unplugged
   Your catalogue is kept. Films show "missing" until the drive is
   back and you scan again.

Back up your catalogue
   Library & Settings > Backup > "Export JSON". Keep that file safe.
   (Or copy the folder  %LOCALAPPDATA%\Film Tagger\data  while the
   app is stopped. It holds films.db and your custom posters.)


---------------------------------------------------------------------
STOP / UNINSTALL
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Stop:       Library & Settings > "Quit Film Tagger"
            (or double-click "Stop Film Tagger.bat").
Uninstall:  Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Film Tagger >
            Uninstall (or Start menu > Film Tagger > Uninstall).
            It asks whether to delete your library (database and custom
            posters). The answer "No" is the default: your library is kept
            in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Film Tagger\data and is used again if you
            reinstall. Your video files are never touched.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
ALTERNATIVE - manual install with your own Python
---------------------------------------------------------------------
If you prefer not to use the Setup program: install Python 3 from
https://www.python.org/downloads/ (tick "Add python.exe to PATH"),
unzip the Film Tagger source zip into a permanent folder and
double-click  "Install Film Tagger.bat". In that setup your catalogue
is the file films.db in that folder. "Stop Film Tagger.bat" and
"Uninstall Film Tagger.bat" in the same folder stop it and remove its
shortcuts.


---------------------------------------------------------------------
MAC / LINUX
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Install Python 3, then in a terminal inside the film-tagger folder:
      python3 -m pip install -r requirements.txt
      python3 app.py
and open http://localhost:5050 in your browser.
(The folder "Browse..." button needs tkinter. If it's missing, just
type the folder path.)
